---
title: Doghood
pubDate: 2025-12-19
---

Doghood,<br>
Where sacrifice is the most blessed architect of all,<br>
And their antler ballet is excruciatingly beautiful,<br>
Tearing the sky wide apart,<br>
Even they are not spared the hunt,<br>
And I endure the piercing that cleans me,<br>
Retinas veiled with lace and dressed in clear water,<br>
With hair in my face, I am rested and seen,<br>
And I lay still while faith kneels,<br>
To be embraced by tradition,<br>
I struggle to keep my bark castled,<br>
As the wind threads the brown fur,<br>
And rushes through my body and ribs,<br>
It empties the woods through me,<br>
Past the river’s stretch,<br>
In the ravine where sight softens,<br>
Between the silver birch and a thirsting deer,<br>
I am a dog willed to bear,<br>
and I will keep what I was given.<br>
Manhood.<br>
